How it works

Five small actions. One useful record

Turn a single dot into a clear path forward

01

Create your wheel

Choose the areas that matter to you: health, work, money, relationships, calm, confidence, or anything else. This becomes your structure, so you are not forced into someone else’s idea of a balanced life.

02

Set focus

Pick one or two areas to pay attention to. Everything can't be fixed at once.

03

Do a quick check-in

Rate each area from 1 to 10. It takes about two minutes and gives you a snapshot of how things actually feel right now.

04

Add a short reflection

Write a few lines about what stood out. What affected the scores. What you want to remember later.

05

See patterns over time

Your check-ins turn into a timeline. You begin to notice what improves, what drops under pressure, and what keeps repeating.
