New and Exciting

Create unlimited wheels, save results, track progress over time

1. Create your wheel

Create Wheels that matter to you, such as Fitness Tracker, Financial Well-being, or Professional Growth.

2. Set focus

Pick one or two areas you plan to focus on over the coming weeks or months, and keep an eye on how your focus shifts.

3. Add a short reflection

Add a few notes to each session about what affected your scores or what you want to remember later.

4. See patterns over time

Begin to notice what improves, what drops under pressure, and what keeps repeating.

Try it for yourself

It takes a few minutes to start. If the structure clicks, you will keep using it. If not, you have at least spent less than on an indifferent sandwich.
