Shortly about myself

My name is John. I'm an Angular Front-End Developer, or, putting it in a human language - I make websites.

About Wheel of Life

I've created Wheel of Life for a big community of people, who tend to reflect on their life, try to make conscious decisions or simply love studying.

As you've probably noticed there weren't any updates over the last few years. Unfortunately, having a day job, stops me from doing any work on the website, simply because it can easily become a second full-time job, which would totally ruin a work-life-happiness balance, we all strive for. At the same time, I seriously dislike that this project, which I really care for, is being put on such a long stand-by.

Reasons

Here I'm not just to moan, but explain why I made this, a long postponed, decision to add a DONATE button.

  • I hope that receiving a real confirmation from real people that this wee website actually does some good, will open more opportunities for me to grow it further.
  • The website gets some heavy traffic from time to time, which does cost quite some money. It'd be nice to share the cost of hosting, domain, etc, with someone else.
  • People have always been the main source of inspiration for me. I'd like to know more about what you'd like to see here, which features are more important, how can I help you in general.

So, having said all that - please, do donate, every penny will be spent to make the website better.
