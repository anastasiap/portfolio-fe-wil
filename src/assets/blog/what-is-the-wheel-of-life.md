# What Is the Wheel of Life?

The Wheel of Life is a circular chart divided into segments, each representing a key area of a person’s life — such as Health, Career, Relationships, Finances, and more. Clients rate their satisfaction in each area on a scale (usually 1–10), and the result is a “wheel” that instantly reveals imbalance or areas needing attention.

### Why Coaches Use It (And Clients Love It)

**Clarity in 15 Minutes**: Clients see their life on one page — visually and objectively.

**Non-confrontational Start**: Especially for new clients, it's a low-barrier entry to deeper conversations.

**Goal Anchor**: Each segment becomes a prompt for coaching goals that actually matter to the client.

**Re-Assessment Tool**: Repeat the exercise every 3–6 months to track change and progress.

### When to Introduce It in Coaching

You can use the Wheel in multiple ways depending on your workflow:

| **Timing**         | **Use Case**                                      |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Session 1          | Icebreaker, values exploration, onboarding intake |
| Quarterly Check-in | Visual progress tracking and goal re-alignment    |
| Stuck Moments      | When the client feels "off" but can’t explain why |
| Closure Sessions   | Reviewing progress and preparing next steps       |

### Customizing the Wheel for Your Clients

**Choose the Right Categories:** Not everyone relates to “Finances” or “Spirituality.” You can edit categories to better match your client’s world — e.g.:

_A founder might include:_ Product Vision, Team Culture, Burnout, Fundraising

_A student might use:_ Academic Pressure, Friendships, Identity, Purpose

_A new parent might add:_ Energy, Support System, Self-Image

Tip: Our interactive version lets you customize all labels, colors, and number of segments.

### Ask Deeper Questions per Segment

Once the ratings are filled, ask:

- What does a 10 look like in this area?
- What would need to change for it to move from 5 to 7?
- Which area drains the most energy right now?
- Which one energizes you?
