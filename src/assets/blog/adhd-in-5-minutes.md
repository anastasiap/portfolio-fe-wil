# How to Know in 5 Minutes If You Should Explore ADHD With a Specialist

ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder) is one of the most discussed conditions in recent years. Many adults notice difficulties with focus, organization, or impulsivity and quietly wonder: *Could this be ADHD, or just everyday distraction?*

Let’s be clear: **only a qualified professional can diagnose ADHD.** But before you book an appointment, you can use a quick self-reflection to see if certain patterns resonate with your everyday life. Think of it less as a test and more as a way to map your challenges and decide if a deeper conversation is worth having.

We call this the **Wheel of Attention**. In just five minutes, you can score yourself across several categories and get a bird’s-eye view of where the struggles cluster.

---

## 🔍 8 Categories for Quick Self-Reflection

Rate each area from 0 (no issue) to 10 (very strong). This is not about labels, but about noticing patterns.

### 1. Focus and Concentration
Do you often start a task but quickly drift away? Is it hard to finish reading an email or document without losing track?

### 2. Organization and Planning
Do deadlines slip through your fingers? Do you often misplace documents, items, or details?

### 3. Impulsivity
Do you tend to interrupt conversations, make quick decisions, or shop impulsively?

### 4. Everyday Memory
Do you forget where you put your keys or what you planned to do five minutes ago?

### 5. Emotional Regulation
Do emotions sometimes overwhelm you to the point where it’s hard to continue working or talking?

### 6. Motivation and Finishing Tasks
Do you leave many projects unfinished? Do you lose interest halfway through?

### 7. Sleep and Energy
Do you struggle with falling asleep? Do you feel constant fatigue or unpredictable bursts of energy?

### 8. Social Interactions
Do relationships get strained because of forgetfulness, disorganization, or impulsivity?

## 🧭 What to Do With the Result

If several of these categories scored high for you, it might be time to bring this up with a specialist. Even if it’s not ADHD, this reflection highlights areas where productivity strategies, new habits, or coaching could make everyday life easier.
