
# Wheel of Life: 5 Sets of Categories for Coaching, Teams, and Projects

**The Wheel of Life isn’t just for life coaches.**  
It’s a flexible visual framework that helps people and teams see where they are and where they want to grow. Whether you're coaching an individual, onboarding graduates, running a medical training program, or reviewing a large-scale project, the **principle is always the same**:

> **Make it visual. Keep it honest. Let the conversation follow.**

But the *categories* you use?  
That’s where the real customization begins.

Below are five powerful category sets, ready to use, remix, or personalize, along with a **free printable PDF** and access to a **live interactive demo**:

## For Life Coaching

Use this classic structure to spark reflection and growth in 1-on-1 coaching sessions. It's ideal for clarity-seeking clients who want to move forward but don’t know where to start.

| Area             | What it Includes                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Health**        | Wellbeing, appearance, nutrition, daily routines, physical activity, sleep       |
| **Relationships** | Communication, intimacy, family dynamics, friendship quality                    |
| **People**        | Support system: siblings, colleagues, allies, even sources of tension           |
| **Vocation**      | Job, career path, business role, sense of purpose, public status                |
| **Finances**      | Income, expenses, savings habits, living conditions                             |
| **Self-Improvement** | Learning, personal development, therapy, skill-building                     |
| **Life Richness** | Fun, hobbies, creative outlets, travel, personal freedom                        |
| **Spiritual**     | Inner compass: faith, creative practices, reflection, art                        |

**Why it works:** Offers a full-spectrum view of life, including often-ignored areas like creativity and social energy. Great for revealing imbalance and hidden frustration.

## For Career Coaching

Designed for those navigating the workplace — changing jobs, seeking promotion, or rebuilding motivation. Use these dimensions to audit alignment between *career identity* and *daily reality*.

| Area             | What it Includes                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Role Clarity**  | Are responsibilities clear? Does the job match the title?                        |
| **Skills**        | Current strengths, performance gaps, upskilling opportunities                    |
| **Learning**      | Access to growth: courses, mentoring, feedback loops                             |
| **Leadership**    | Influence, delegation, decision-making capacity                                 |
| **Network**       | Peers, mentors, communities, professional tribe                                  |
| **Recognition**   | Feedback, appreciation, visibility, acknowledgment                              |
| **Balance**       | Time management, boundaries, sustainable effort                                  |
| **Motivation**    | Engagement, internal drive, sense of mission                                    |

**Why it works:** Helps clarify misalignment between ambition and context — or reveal that it’s not the role, but the *ecosystem* that needs a shift.

## For Wellbeing Check-ins

This version centers on *health and energy*, perfect for use in personal journaling, wellness programs, or employee support conversations. Focus is placed on sustainability rather than optimization.

| Area             | What it Includes                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Sleep**         | Quality, consistency, recovery cycles                                            |
| **Energy**        | Mental clarity, daily rhythm, fatigue levels                                     |
| **Nutrition**     | Food choices, hydration, patterns (not calories)                                |
| **Stress**        | Triggers, emotional load, work-life spillover                                   |
| **Recovery**      | Breaks, vacations, rest culture                                                  |
| **Movement**      | Daily activity, flexibility, strength, mobility                                 |
| **Mindset**       | Mental framing, resilience, self-talk                                           |
| **Support**       | Trusted connections, emotional safety net, therapy access                       |

**Why it works:** Wellbeing is multidimensional — and often one key shift (like improving sleep) creates a ripple effect across other areas.

## For Team Assessments

This structure is built for *team retrospectives, workshops, or performance reviews*. It provides a shared language to reflect without blame — and supports productive dialogue in flat or hierarchical teams.

| Area             | What it Includes                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Collaboration** | Daily cooperation, helpfulness, working styles                                   |
| **Communication** | Frequency, clarity, tone, listening culture                                     |
| **Goals**         | Are we aligned? Are objectives visible and shared?                              |
| **Roles**         | Who owns what? Are boundaries respected?                                        |
| **Resources**     | Access to time, tools, and decision-makers                                      |
| **Decision-making** | Speed, inclusiveness, transparency                                             |
| **Trust**         | Accountability, reliability, psychological safety                              |
| **Motivation**    | Collective energy, ownership, emotional buy-in                                 |

**Why it works:** This version makes invisible dynamics visible. Use it to initiate action — not just reflection.

## For Project Reviews

Perfect for *retrospectives* and *post-mortems*, this format blends delivery metrics with human insight. It moves beyond the Gantt chart to explore *what actually worked, and why (or why not)*.

| Area             | What it Includes                                                                 |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Clarity of Goals** | Is everyone aligned? Do we agree on what success looks like?                 |
| **Planning**      | Feasibility, realism, pivot-readiness                                            |
| **Tools**         | Suitability of platforms, processes, and workflows                              |
| **Risk Handling** | How were problems anticipated or managed?                                       |
| **Stakeholder Alignment** | Cross-functional buy-in, communication channels                        |
| **Outcomes**      | Delivery quality, feature completion, team morale                               |
| **Value**         | Actual business/user impact — not just “did we ship”                            |
| **Lessons Learned** | Concrete takeaways, process improvements, mindset shifts                     |

**Why it works:** Turns vague “how did that go?” into a structured, honest debrief. Ideal for agile teams, cross-functional leads, or solo reflection.
