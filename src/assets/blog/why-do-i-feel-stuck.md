# Feeling Stuck? Try This 10-Minute Self-Reflection Tool That Actually Shows You Why

**No journal. No therapist. Just a simple circle that brings clarity.**

We’ve all had those days or weeks, when everything feels *off*. You’re not exactly unhappy, but you’re not excited either. You have things to do, goals to chase, maybe even people to love... and yet, there’s this vague weight in your chest and a question in your mind:

**“Why do I feel stuck?”**

Instead of doom-scrolling, venting to a friend (again), or forcing yourself to power through, try this:

It’s called the **Wheel of Life**.  
And it might be the most helpful 10 minutes you’ll spend today.

### What Is the Wheel of Life?

It’s a **visual self-assessment tool** — basically a circle divided into slices, like a pie. Each slice represents one area of your life (think: Health, Finances, Relationships, Work, Fun, etc.). You simply rate your satisfaction in each area from 1 to 10.

Then you connect the dots.  
The result? A snapshot of your *real* life balance — not what you think, but what you actually feel.

### How It Works in 3 Steps

1. **Choose Your Categories**  
   Pick 8–12 areas that feel important right now. Some ideas:  
   `Career – Health – Sleep – Social Life – Creativity – Romance – Finances – Personal Growth`

2. **Rate Each One from 1 to 10**

- 1 = totally off track
- 10 = fully satisfied

3. **Look at the Shape of Your Wheel**

- Is it lopsided? Empty in some areas? Surprisingly full in others?
- That’s your life talking.

### Why It Works (Even If You Hate Self-Help)

- **No fluff. No journaling.** You don’t have to write your life story.
- **It’s visual.** Seeing imbalance is sometimes more powerful than talking about it.
- **It’s fast.** 10 minutes is all it takes — a quiet moment on a Sunday night, or a mental check-in on your lunch break.

### What You'll Learn About Yourself

Most people walk away from this exercise with at least one of these insights:

- *“No wonder I feel stuck. I’ve ignored my social life for 6 months.”*
- *“I thought my job was the issue. But really, I’m just exhausted.”*
- *“I actually feel okay. I just forgot to notice it.”*

The Wheel helps you connect the dots between how you spend your time and how you feel.

### Real Talk: What Do You *Do* With the Results?

Here are a few ideas:

- Pick **one slice of the wheel** to focus on for the next week — no pressure to fix everything.
- Share it with a coach, friend, or partner if you're craving support.
- Revisit it next month and see what’s changed.
